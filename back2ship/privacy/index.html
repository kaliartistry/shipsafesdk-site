This policy describes the privacy-updated beta prepared on 23 October 2026. Earlier beta builds may have default-on diagnostic logging and different backup rules. The updated build will be identified in its release notes.

Back2Ship demonstration beta privacy policy
Updated 23 October 2026
Operator: Kali McCarthy (Kali Artistry). Contact: kalimccarthy@gmail.com.

Location and navigation
Back2Ship uses precise location and heading for return-to-ship and excursion guidance. Real location access is blocked until you accept the location disclosure, and Android/iOS permission is also required. Active tracking and its location notification can continue while you use other apps or lock the screen. Stop tracking or change device permission to stop access. This demonstration is offered outside France; the invitation link is not a geographic block.

Optional test records
Diagnostic Session Logging is off until you choose it. An upgrade resets the earlier beta's default-on choice to off. If enabled while tracking starts, CSV/JSON records can contain precise GPS, motion/events, times, battery and trip information, including cabin number if entered. Switching Session Logging off ends the active diagnostic session. Existing logs are preserved; use Sessions to delete or export them. Startup cleanup attempts to remove session files older than 90 days. Separate walk recordings are explicitly started/stopped by you, have a 14-day/30-walk retention limit checked when the app is used, and provide delete controls. Cleanup does not run continuously while the app is closed.

Storage and backups
Cruise, emergency/medical notes and tracking-resume records use platform-protected secure storage. Diagnostic session files and walk recordings are exportable files, protected by the app/device storage boundary rather than by the record-store encryption. Android backup rules exclude both session logs and walk recordings from cloud backup/device transfer. Older external backups and copies you already exported are outside this change. Other app data follows your device's backup settings.

Maps and external services
The map uses Mapbox and downloads online resources. Mapbox documents location and usage telemetry by default when the app gathers location. Review the map information/attribution control for Mapbox's telemetry choice. Mapbox has its own privacy information and retention/deletion practices; offline routing does not mean every SDK datum stays on your device. We do not claim that all telemetry is irreversibly anonymous. This app does not directly upload your cruise or medical records to a ShipSafe server in this beta. PIR and heat map are incomplete, and their live services and the staged meetup feature are disabled in this build.

Your choices and exports
Location permission, Stop tracking, the optional Session Logging switch, and saved session/walk deletion controls are available. Export/Share hands selected files to the app or recipient you choose; they can reveal location, time and trip/cabin details. Deleting the original does not delete those copies. External map/telephone actions use your chosen app/service. Clearing cruise data is not an all-record erasure operation. For other privacy or deletion questions, contact the operator above; Mapbox-held information is also governed by its policy. No Back2Ship account creation is required.
